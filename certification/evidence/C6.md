# C6 Certification Evidence — M1 Rules & Game Integrity

Candidate: **1.0.0-rc.7**

## Goal

M1 means:

> The shipped rules engine plays the product's supported form of Go legally and
> preserves game state correctly under adversarial and randomized use.

This is a rules-engine certification. It does not claim that KataGo is live,
that a beginner understands the rules, or that the app implements every
national tournament ruleset.

## Supported rules profile

Independent play uses the product's documented area-scoring rules:

- Black and White alternate legal plays;
- occupied intersections are illegal;
- suicide is forbidden;
- captures are resolved before suicide is evaluated;
- default ko is simple ko;
- the headless engine also supports positional superko;
- pass is always permitted while the game is active;
- two consecutive passes move Independent Play into dead-group confirmation;
- disputed life/death can resume play;
- confirmed dead groups are removed before final area scoring;
- White receives configured komi;
- captures are tracked for UI/history but are not added again under area
  scoring.

This is intentionally described as the **THIEPN area rules profile**, not as a
claim of exact Chinese/AGA/Japanese tournament-rule identity.

## Defects found during C6

### 1. Conflicting SGF setup edits could silently overwrite

A study node could contain setup instructions for the same point in more than
one of:

- AB (add Black);
- AW (add White);
- AE (add empty).

Replay applied them sequentially, so the last write silently won.

That violates the project rule that malformed/imported study state must fail
explicitly rather than fabricate a position.

**Fix:** conflicting setup edits now throw before the board is mutated.

### 2. Unsupported SGF geometry could be silently coerced

Examples such as:

- `SZ[1]`;
- `SZ[26]`;
- `SZ[9.5]`;
- `SZ[9:13]`;
- malformed size text;

could previously be rounded, clamped, or partially interpreted.

**Fix:** SGF board geometry is now strict:

- integer dimensions only;
- 2 through 25;
- square boards only;
- `SZ[n:n]` accepted;
- rectangular boards rejected explicitly.

### 3. Explicit non-Go SGF game types were ignored

The parser accepted GM but did not reject `GM[2]` or another explicit
non-Go game type.

**Fix:** explicit `GM` values other than `1` are rejected.

## Deterministic randomized game campaign

`src/go/engine/certification.test.ts` executes positional-superko legal-game
campaigns over:

- 5×5: 12 deterministic seeds;
- 9×9: 8 deterministic seeds;
- 13×13: 4 deterministic seeds;
- 19×19: 2 deterministic seeds.

Each campaign exercises up to dozens/hundreds of legal plies and continuously
checks:

- immutable failure behavior for illegal attempts;
- board dimensions;
- board-history length;
- current board hash equals the final history hash;
- every on-board group has at least one liberty;
- stone conservation;
- capture-count conservation;
- Black/White turn parity;
- play-created positional-superko positions are not duplicates;
- two-pass termination;
- play/pass after game end is rejected.

## Adversarial corpus

Focused tests cover:

- single- and multi-stone captures;
- simultaneous capture of disconnected adjacent enemy groups;
- suicide rejection;
- capture-before-suicide legality;
- immediate simple-ko rejection;
- simple-ko recapture after intervening plays;
- positional-superko rejection of an older repeated board;
- distinction between positional superko and simple ko;
- occupied and out-of-bounds moves;
- setup overlap;
- pass termination;
- post-game move rejection.

## Scoring invariants

Random board partitions verify:

```
black stones
+ white stones
+ black territory
+ white territory
+ neutral points
= board area
```

With zero komi, swapping Black/White on the same geometry must swap the two
area totals.

The end-game layer additionally verifies:

- dead-group toggling acts on the whole connected group;
- dead-stone preview never mutates the original board;
- confirmed dead stones are removed before final area scoring.

## SGF replay equivalence

Generated legal 9×9, 13×13, and 19×19 games are converted through:

```
SavedGameRecord
→ StudyDocument
→ SGF serialization
→ SGF parser
→ engine replay
```

The replayed result must preserve:

- final serialized board;
- capture totals;
- move type;
- move color;
- move coordinates.

This catches drift between Independent Play, Study, SGF import/export, and the
headless rules engine.

## Remaining boundary

M1 certification applies to supported product rules and saved-game/study
round-trips.

It does not claim:

- exact Japanese territory-rule adjudication;
- every national superko variant;
- rectangular boards;
- automatic dead-group classification;
- live KataGo legality authority.

KataGo remains advisory and is never allowed to override the P1 rules engine.

## Decision

C6 may be marked certified only when:

- the dedicated C6 workflow passes;
- the complete repository test suite passes;
- build/release checks pass on the same candidate head;
- no C0/C1 rules-integrity defect remains open.

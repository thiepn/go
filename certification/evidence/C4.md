# C4 Certification Evidence — Physical Mobile & PWA

Candidate: **1.0.0-rc.5**

## Why rc.5 exists

C4 found a real mobile-display defect in rc.4.

The document opted into `viewport-fit=cover`, but global layout containment only
used the left and right safe-area insets. Installed iPhone/iPad web apps can
therefore place important UI inside top or bottom display-obstruction regions.

rc.5 adds all four safe-area insets and keeps the usable app height bounded
inside those insets.

## C4 implementation

- four-edge safe-area variables based on `env(safe-area-inset-*)`;
- safe viewport-height calculation for `vh` and `dvh`;
- top-level app containment inside the safe viewport;
- browser-versus-installed launch-mode detection;
- iOS `navigator.standalone` compatibility;
- learner-visible Launch mode status in Device & accessibility;
- dedicated production-mode C4 Playwright configuration;
- Android-phone Chromium emulation;
- iPhone WebKit emulation;
- iPad WebKit emulation;
- synthetic notch/home-indicator safe-area test;
- portrait→landscape reflow test;
- service-worker-controlled offline reload in browser emulation;
- durable local progress across offline cold launch;
- 9×9, 13×13, and 19×19 touch-placement checks;
- Precision Zoom checks for 13×13 and 19×19;
- physical-device evidence checklist for Android, iPhone, iPad, and desktop
  browser families.

## Evidence policy

Browser emulation is supporting evidence only.

C4 is not physically certified until the required rows in
`certification/c4-device-checklist.json` have been executed on real hardware
and evidence has been recorded. In particular, emulation does not prove:

- real OS Add to Home Screen/install UX;
- actual notch/Dynamic Island/home-indicator rendering;
- real touch precision and accidental gesture behavior;
- hardware haptics;
- OS task switching/background eviction;
- storage behavior under the installed iOS web-app container;
- browser/OS update behavior.
